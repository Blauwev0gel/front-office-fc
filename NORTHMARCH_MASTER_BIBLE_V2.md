# NORTHMARCH — Master Bible V2.0
Status: consolidated design baseline, owner authorised redesign; individual novel choices proposed for review. 28 November 2026. Active work target: prototype/de-spelmaker.html on claude/loving-goodall-uv00vn. V1 retained as historical reference; no branch migration or release implied. If adopted, V2 supersedes V1 for this prototype only. Research 01–10 remain in root, unchanged.
## 1. North star
Found a football club, build an institution, leave a history. You are chairman, not coach. A living football world and autonomous people create opportunities and constraints. Every significant decision has a cause, tradeoff, execution by someone, delayed outcome and evidence. No guaranteed success or compulsory busywork.
## 2. Product
Retro football management, readable mobile-first text, club colours, portraits, league tables and newspaper. Inspiration: FM world, Crusader Kings people, FC PRO 2 progression, No.10 Full Confidence decisions, Hometown FC locality; design lenses, not cloned mechanics. Single player, offline-capable prototype, no paid AI dependency.
## 3. Start and role
Found a member-owned club in lowest division, choose name/town/colours, founding priorities with tradeoffs, initial resources. Four functional portfolios Football, Academy, Commercial, Finance; retain current four initial heads for first playable slice, but allow combined roles and part-time/volunteer support in later approved migration. Two candidates per head initially. Chairman sets objectives, appoints leaders, allocates resources, initiates research, convenes meetings, approves exceptional commitments and represents club. Coach selects team/tactics, TD recruits. Chairman may approve exceptional financial deals, not routinely pick players. Existing club starts and alternative ownership deferred.
## 4. Three loops
Weekly: read material inbox/news, inspect match and club, optionally initiate or intervene, Continue. Strategic: choose ambition, commission work, compare options, delegate, observe milestones, revise and review. Career: seasonal budget, windows, sporting race, club development, ALV, evolving identity and history. All share same state. No action-point quota; time, capacity, cost and authority constrain actions. Quiet weeks valid. No artificial drama.
## 5. Core systems
Football: preserve existing match and transfer engine initially, audit world coverage; five divisions of sixteen and cup are target, not claim current implementation. Transfer window uses existing day clock and stops for material exceptions; all weeks simulated. Financial: cash, dated receipts/payments, commitments, restricted funds, reserve and forecast; simple UI with optional detail. No automatic borrowing or budget creation. Organisation: role coverage, capacity and competence; four tiles, staff autonomy via persistent plans. Development: facilities provide capacity only when operated and demanded; feasibility, permission, finance, member approval, milestones and maintenance. People: skill, philosophy, ambition, relationship label and memories; identity evolves through material actions, culture distinct from reputation. Governance: members own club, annual election, consent for major projects (retain current >25% turnover rule pending denominator clarification), credible failure and recovery. World: other clubs and local partners act with finite resources at appropriate fidelity.
## 6. Information
One hub, Continue always available with documented defaults, decisions from named people, reports/news separate. Persistent dossier links cause, options, commitment, milestones, outcome. Department controls within department tile. No new screen without meaningful decision or useful evidence. Weekly newspaper summarises football and club changes; not a mandatory popup. Match remains visible but not tactical gameplay.
## 7. Progression and failure
No fixed upgrade ladder, no highscore, no guaranteed promotion. Success can be local identity, youth pathway, financial resilience or sporting achievement. Staff and facilities cost money and time. Relegation, rejected projects, failed recruitment, financial distress and removal at ALV are meaningful outcomes. No arbitrary reset or rescue; offer plausible restructuring. Dynasty/family deferred until base game validated.
## 8. Architecture
One state, clock, seeded RNG, command gateway, finance ledger, feasibility contract, typed events and serialisable dossiers. Keep single HTML deliverable initially; logical module boundaries, incremental extraction only when needed. No rewrite or second engine. UI reads projections and dispatches commands. Existing save migration tested before release. See architecture/SYSTEM_ARCHITECTURE_V2.md.
## 9. Development
First complete vertical slice: founding → budget → autonomous sponsor renewal/alternative → chairman initiative → dated cash and capacity effects → season football → ALV. Then football/academy pathway, organisational evolution, infrastructure/governance, identity and external world. Every phase playable and locally tested; human playtest early. See development/ROADMAP_V2.md.
## 10. Decision authority and change control
V2 design baseline proposed under owner's permission to consolidate; do not claim owner individually approved every new mechanic. Record changes in design/DECISION_CATALOGUE_V2.md. V1 and research are evidence, not parallel canon after V2 adoption. No automatic changes to slimline-main, Pages, CLAUDE.md or AGENTS.md. No heavy CI without permission. Distinguish Designed, Implemented, Tested, Playtested, Validated, Released.
