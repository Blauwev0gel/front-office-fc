## Role
Chairman controls priorities, mandates, leadership, structural investment, risk appetite, governance and relationships. Coach controls tactics/selection. TD handles individual recruitment within mandate. Chair may intervene in exceptional deals under existing canon, not compulsory player-by-player management.
## Agency catalogue
C01 initiate strategic review; C02 set annual goal and review date; C03 chair cross-department meeting; C04 commission research; C05 alter mandate; C06 recruit or replace head; C07 review coach through TD; C08 approve exceptional spend; C09 set capital pipeline; C10 negotiate external partnership at strategic level; C11 engage members; C12 restructure during crisis; C13 choose not to act; C14 challenge report assumptions; C15 follow up prior commitment.
All actions accessible from relevant department or existing meeting/inbox, with cost/lead time/authority, expected outcomes and evidence. No new screen per action. No artificial weekly action quota; existing meeting cooldown stays.
## Cadence and agenda
Weekly continue: autonomous activity and match; timely exceptions. Monthly optional review, quarterly plan checkpoint, seasonal budget and ALV. Chairman can always inspect status and schedule feasible work even if inbox empty. Meeting record: agenda, participants, viewpoints, decision, owner, due date, next review, outcome. A meeting that cannot change policy or commitment should be a report instead.
## Example
Academy wants pathway, coach prioritises survival, TD proposes two options. Chair agrees six-month pathway target without choosing players, or postpones with explicit review date. Staff decide execution; finance budgets support. Review shows youth minutes, progression and results against baseline, not guaranteed improvement. Trust depends on agreement and observed follow-through.
## Baseline gap
A.meet static options, A.mandate, A.replace, A.coachReview exist. Need persistent chair agenda, cross-dept commitments, alternative plans and follow-up; do not delete working actions. Implement one joint academy/football dossier with optional initiation and review.
## Validation
Empty inbox still permits strategic initiative; decline/deferral valid; no forced actions per week; comparable decisions have distinct outcomes over seasons; autonomous coach remains in control. Track voluntary versus forced decisions, commitment follow-through, repeated proposals, and player explanation accuracy in playtest. No evidence of exact ideal decision frequency; test rather than prescribe.
