# Research 06 — People & Organisational DNA
Advisory v1. MB-47 identity/culture and MB-46 simple relationship display binding.
## Evidence
Brentford long-term squad strategy and staff recruitment (03 sources); FC United 2021 structure/member survey and 2024 volunteer-board reflection (01 S07/S11); Norway quality club interviews (01 S04) and mixed German/Swiss sport volunteering (01 S05). These support role continuity, competing priorities and routines, not numerical personality effects. Existing R03/R04/R09 coach fit, succession, identity constraints.
## Separate constructs
Founding origin: town/name/history fixed; identity: youth/community/commercial/results priorities slowly change through repeated material actions; culture: prudence/ambition/innovation changes through leadership behaviour and outcomes; reputation: external beliefs by stakeholder; person: competence, philosophy, ambition, reliability, relationships and memories. Do not collapse into one morale meter.
## Simulation
Person skill influences forecast quality, execution reliability and learning; philosophy influences option preferences; ambition influences career demand; trust changes after commitments and fair treatment. Keep visible relationship label Loyal/Warm/Neutral/Cool/Hostile plus trust% per MB-46. Store bounded event memories with cause and age, not hidden omniscience. Candidate selection trades fit, skill, wages and availability. Succession records role requirements, shortlist, handover and knowledge coverage; vacancies reduce effective capacity.
Identity update proposal: exponentially weighted history of consequential actions, with annual cap and threshold; do not change for repeated clicks or speeches. Synthetic alpha .05 per material event, cap annual shift pending testing; no empirical support. Separate internal values and public reputation; public promises can diverge from behaviour. Culture affects willingness to propose risk and share information, never forces all staff to agree.
## Scenarios and decisions
P01 hire strong but misaligned head vs aligned less experienced candidate; P02 retain under external offer or prepare successor; P03 clarify competing departmental philosophies; P04 respond to missed commitment; P05 develop staff through training/mentoring; P06 manage loss of volunteer expertise; P07 change strategy with explanation; P08 protect identity against profitable incompatible offer. No demographic stereotypes or personality determinism.
## Gap and implementation
Current candidates/traits, simple relationships, result identity and culture adjustments exist. Add event provenance, slow identity update, institutional handover and candidate market only after autonomy works. Preserve existing founding DNA and display. First slice: coach review with actual trajectory, fit and alternative succession; staff departure retains club records.
## Tests
Same skill different philosophy → different feasible choices; same philosophy different skill → different estimates/outcomes; no trust farming by repetitive meetings; identity stable after isolated loss; repeated meaningful action changes reputation over seasons; replacement doesn't erase dossiers. Four five-season club paths distinguishable. No psychological scale validation; research is qualitative and cases football-specific plus adjacent sport.
