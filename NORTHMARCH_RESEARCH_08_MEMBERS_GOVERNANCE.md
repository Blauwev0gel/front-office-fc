# Research 08 — Members & Governance
Advisory v1; existing MB-47 fictional member club and ALV are binding.
## Evidence
Dons Trust https://thedonstrust.org/about-us/ uses one member one vote and annual elections. AFC Wimbledon fan engagement https://images.gc.afcwimbledonservices.co.uk/1c6c1990-4f4c-11ef-8578-93764c54e58f.pdf describes minimum three general meetings annually and consultation on debt/stadium. Club board https://www.afcwimbledon.co.uk/club/afcw-plc/ is over 75% trust controlled; distinct operating entity. FC United rules https://fc-utd.co.uk/files/documents/Rules-of-FC-United-of-Manchester.pdf describe revocable delegation. UK fan-led review https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/fan-led-review-of-football-governance-securing-the-games-future/fan-led-review-of-football-governance-securing-the-games-future emphasises board responsibility, transparency and risk.
## Model
Members are owners, not uniform fans. Separate member count, participation, priorities, trust, financial commitment, and public supporter sentiment. Aggregate cohorts (affordability/community, youth, sporting ambition, financial stability) with overlapping priorities; no fixed real-world weights. Existing ALV appoints/removes chair under canon. Board and committees represented only where canon permits; do not import UK corporate law.
## Decisions
M01 annual mandate; M02 membership pricing; M03 consult on contentious sponsor; M04 explain budget variance; M05 propose member financing; M06 capital approval above canon threshold; M07 respond to failed mandate; M08 election campaign/strategic programme; M09 negotiate compromises; M10 resign or accept removal under existing rules.
## Voting proposal
For each eligible cohort estimate support from goal alignment, perceived financial risk, trust and evidence quality; turnout separately; aggregate weighted votes, show uncertainty before vote. Threshold/quorum exclusively canonical; no new vote types without approval. Synthetic test fixture: 100 eligible, 60 vote, 35 yes/25 no, majority of votes cast passes if applicable; not proposed legal rule. No vote reroll or bribery mechanic. Decisions rejected remain blocked until materially revised.
## Dynamics
Members join on identity, accessibility and sporting/community experience; leave for cost, disengagement or distrust. Cash contributions not guaranteed; record terms in finance. Broken promises reduce credibility; transparency may reduce surprise but not erase bad performance. Opposition can be constructive. Annual election outcome reflects tenure not last match alone.
## Gap/slice/tests
ALV/member unrest exists; richer mandate persistence and heterogeneous preferences not demonstrated. First slice: stadium dossier with consultation, options, financial range, recorded vote, post-vote accountability. Preserve existing approval rules and chair election. Test refusal blocks project, low turnout handled, no duplicate votes, member funds restricted where relevant, strategic promises persist, profitable unpopular options have tradeoffs. No representative member preference data; do not use isolated survey as universal electorate. No simulation performed.
