# Research 09 — External Football World
Advisory v1; retain current football engine and fictional Northmarch.
## Evidence base
Brentford recruitment planning https://www.brentfordfc.com/en/news/the-long-read-brentford-director-of-football-phil-giles-transfers supports contract/age succession and market availability. UEFA youth and personnel requirements (01 S17–19) apply only to eligible competitions. IFR authority guidance (04) and FIFA feasibility (05) show external governance and planning constraints. Existing real-world catalogue R06–R10 covers member constraints, trading and land. No new representative transfer dataset retrieved; FIFA Global Transfer Report portal https://inside.fifa.com/transfer-system/global-transfer-report is a follow-up source, not claimed extracted data.
## World model
Club: division, reputation, finances, philosophy, squad needs, contracts, facilities, board risk; staff: role, skill, contract, ambition, availability; market: offers, enquiries, negotiations, deadlines, competing demand; locality: population/demand proxy, sponsors, schools, council/land, rivalries; competition: schedule, rules, distributions, promotion/relegation; news: material observable events. Reuse existing entities not separate duplicate league.
## Causal loops
Other clubs face same budget/capacity constraints at appropriate simulation detail; demand arises from gaps rather than random forced offers. Contracts expire; staff and players move; clubs can reject, negotiate or withdraw. Reputation affects pool and willingness, not guaranteed signings. Promotion changes future revenue and obligations; relegation leaves contracts. Regional sponsor competition and school partnerships have limited capacity. Council permission depends on site and process, not a single relationship score; relationships affect communication, not bypass of law.
## Fidelity tiers
Player club full fidelity; direct rivals and counterparties compact financial/needs state; distant clubs aggregate season transitions. Maintain conservation of transfers and staff identity. Deterministic seed and stable IDs; no heavy per-club weekly LLM. Fictional names, no need real licensed data.
## Chairman decisions
W01 market mandate; W02 exceptional offer; W03 strategic affiliation; W04 respond to competing staff offer; W05 negotiate ground lease; W06 invest in local partnerships; W07 prepare promotion requirements; W08 adapt to rule changes. Staff carry out individual search and deals.
## Quantitative fixture (synthetic)
Five clubs each one open role and three eligible staff candidates; after two sign elsewhere, remaining clubs must revise offer or defer; no duplication. Sponsor pool 6 firms with annual capacity one exclusive contract each; market clearing prevents infinite revenue. Sensitivity on market depth and reputation weights; not empirical.
## Gaps and tests
Current match/transfer/world functions require fresh audit before implementation; baseline review confirms football simulation but not independently verified full external economy. First slice: staff market and sponsor competition linked to existing contracts, then transfer market auditing, locality and news. Tests: no double employment, transfers conserve cash between clubs when both tracked, window rules, external club cash constraints, no infinite sponsors, predictable seeded outcomes, plausible long-run league variation. No new numerical market calibration or runtime tests here.
