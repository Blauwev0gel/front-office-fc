# Game design V2
## First 15 minutes
1 Found club: name, town, colours, priorities; show local demand and financial constraints. 2 Appoint heads: compare philosophy, competence, wages; no hidden mandatory optimal candidate. 3 Boardroom: finance and TD explain actual constraints; choose one seasonal ambition and budget. 4 First meaningful initiative: sponsor renewal or market study, compare feasible alternatives and delegate. 5 Continue: match result, staff progress and newspaper. 6 Follow-up: actual offer or failure, financial consequence, next action. No tutorial-only rules.
## Player verbs
Inspect, initiate, commission, meet, set priority, allocate, delegate, approve, reject, defer, renegotiate mandate, hire/replace, consult, review. Each verb maps to a command and a meaningful state change or information result. No individual player selection.
## Interface
Hub: date, cash/forecast, league position, member confidence, inbox, four department tiles, current initiatives, next match, Continue. Department tile: head, remit, capacity, budget, plan, actual results, actions. Dossier: evidence, alternatives, costs, owner, deadline, history. Newspaper: match, league, material club events. ALV: promise vs outcome, finances, member judgment. Retro typography, readable mobile, no gratuitous modal or new tabs.
## Strategic scenarios
Commercial: renewal vs new category vs defer; sponsor terms affect cash and identity. Football: coach review and succession, not tactics. Academy: school partnership and coaching bottleneck, TD pathway. Finance: cash trough, cost reduction or financing. Infrastructure: lease/share/build, member consent and demand. Every scenario has no-action path, uncertainty, follow-up and opportunity cost.
## Emotional progression
Club book records first match, first homegrown debut, promotion, stadium opening, financial recovery, member election; generated from facts, no bonus farming. Show names and history of people. Match stakes derive from table and ambition. A failed plan is not a scripted disaster; it may create recovery choices.
## Anti-patterns
Fixed weekly choice quota, endless upgrades, scripted crises unrelated to state, per-player chores, omniscient reports, guaranteed returns, fake prototype rules, compulsory long animations. Playtest first season before expanding.
