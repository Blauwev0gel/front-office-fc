A chairman's year, three ways

Each slice follows one part of the job through a whole season, from June to the AGM in May. You commit in June, your people carry it out, the season shows whether you were right, and the members judge you. The real Northmarch world runs underneath: 80 clubs, every match, every pound.

Money · the budget, the half-year figures, the accounts

The books

Sign a budget on a forecast: the course for your director, the season-ticket price, whether to borrow, and what you promise the members. In December the treasurer puts plan against reality. January is your last chance to close the gap.

The ground
