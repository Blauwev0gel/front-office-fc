# Development roadmap V2
## P0 Architecture lock
Refetch branch, inventory actual features and legacy decisions, capture baseline saves, reproduce financial and serialization issues, establish cheap local test harness. Gate: baseline known, migration and rollback plan, no code rewrite.
## P1 First Real Season
Implement minimal dated ledger, shared feasibility, sponsor renewal/alternative plan, chairman initiative, persistent dossier and actual outcome; retain football and ALV. Start with founding club. Gate: one full season human playable, choices alter cash and opportunity, autonomous staff act, no duplicate payments. Stop and iterate if boring.
## P2 Build Your Football Club
Joint academy/football pathway, coach review and recruitment mandate, actual progression and match consequences. Gate: chair influences strategy without selecting players.
## P3 Build Your Organisation
Role coverage, shared capacity, support hire/volunteer/outsourcing, succession and retrenchment. Gate: different organisational structures viable, no mandatory hire ladder.
## P4 Build Your Infrastructure
Feasibility, lease/build/defer, explicit financing, council, member consent, milestone costs and maintenance. Gate: failed approval blocks work, demand determines returns.
## P5 Club Identity
Persistent promises, trust, member cohorts, reputation, culture and club book. Gate: repeated decisions change club over years, ALV grounded in outcomes.
## P6 Living World
Finite external markets, rival clubs, locality and news; audit transfer conservation and world coverage. Gate: counterparties act independently, no infinite resources.
## P7 Complete Game
Balance, accessibility, save migration, performance, human playtests and release. No release without owner approval.
## Working protocol
One phase at a time, small commits, local tests before publication. Every phase has playable artifact, acceptance report, known limitations and rollback. Research gaps collected just-in-time. No heavy GitHub Actions without explicit permission. Keep root research and V1 unchanged; no Pages deployment in consolidation.
